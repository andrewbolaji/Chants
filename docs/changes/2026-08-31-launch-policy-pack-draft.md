## Why this shape

- Separate public-copy boundaries from internal holds, evidence and decisions so incomplete policy promises cannot be mistaken for live behavior.
- Reuse the single approved support address for launch rather than create multiple unstaffed channels. No private support destination or residential address is reproduced. The branded alias is selected but setup remains unverified.
- Preserve the existing direct in-app deletion route. A public page and monitored email request can provide an external entry without a second account system, but secure verification, dispatch into the durable workflow and completion handling still need implementation and proof.
- Do not call attribution replacement deletion or genuine anonymization. Source leaves contribution bodies and published video bytes, and omits account-linked upload-limit cleanup. Record these as launch corrections, not as rights granted by policy prose.
- Keep retention decisions separate from configuration. Unresolved recovery evidence cannot safely expire merely because a draft mentions a period. Provider retention, soft-deleted objects and restored databases have separate boundaries.
- Keep 17+ unchanged while flagging that this admits minors and does not gate public web pages. Country-specific assessment is needed; choosing a minimum age is not evidence of legal compliance.
