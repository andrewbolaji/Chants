Community Rules

Effective 31 August 2026. Funny, brilliant, ridiculous, or a first attempt: football creativity belongs here. Swearing and ordinary rivalry are not automatically abuse. Context matters.

  • Banter is welcome. Hate is not. No racist, discriminatory, or dehumanizing abuse, protected-characteristic slurs, or targeted harassment.
  • No threats or tragedy chanting. Do not encourage violence, celebrate real deaths or disasters, mock victims, or share private information to intimidate someone.
  • No sexual exploitation or explicit sexual content. No non-consensual intimate material, sexual harassment, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or child sexual abuse material, including synthetic material.
  • Respect rights and privacy. Share only material you are entitled to use. Do not pass off another creator's work as your own or feature people in a way that invades their privacy.
  • Keep the competition honest. No scams, impersonation, spam, fake engagement, coordinated false reports, or attempts to evade account restrictions.

How moderation works

New videos are reviewed before publication. Text is not all reviewed in advance. Reports and automated checks can temporarily hide content, and operators can remove content or restrict accounts. Serious safety concerns are prioritized. A report is not guaranteed removal, and approval is not legal clearance.

Use Report for abuse or safety, Suggest an edit for wrong or outdated chant information, and Block to filter a creator in supported signed-in views. Blocking does not erase public content from the internet.

Urgent child safety

Email support@chantsfc.com with the subject “Urgent child safety”. Do not download or forward abusive material. Send its Chants location or ID and a description. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Chants is not an emergency service.

Appeals

If you think we got a decision wrong, email support@chantsfc.com with the content location and your explanation. Do not repost removed material to bypass review.
