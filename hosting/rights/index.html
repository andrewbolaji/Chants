Rights and Takedown

Effective 31 August 2026. Email support@chantsfc.com with the subject “Chants rights request”. Do not attach unnecessary identity documents or sensitive media.

Business correspondence can be sent to Thunderriver Tech LLC, 5667 Treaschwig Rd #1014, Spring, TX 77373, United States. Email remains the fastest way to start a request.

What to send

  1. The exact Chants URL or content ID, plus a useful video timestamp if relevant.
  2. The right, work, or person involved and why you believe the use is unauthorized.
  3. Whether you are the rightsholder, affected person, or an authorized representative.
  4. A safe way to contact you and only the supporting information needed to assess the request.

This route covers copyright, unauthorized footage, impersonation, and privacy concerns. We may ask for missing information, restrict access while reviewing, remove material, or explain why no action is warranted. A complaint does not automatically prove infringement.

US copyright notices

If you are sending a formal US copyright notice, include your signature, identification of the protected work and the allegedly infringing material, sufficient location and contact information, a good-faith statement that the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or law, and a statement that the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the owner.

Formal notices can require contact information to be shared with the contributor or relevant parties. Chants does not claim that this page alone establishes eligibility for any legal safe harbor.

If your contribution was restricted

Email support@chantsfc.com with the subject “Chants moderation appeal”, the content ID, the decision if available, and your explanation. A normal appeal is not a formal legal counter-notice. Do not re-upload restricted material to bypass review.
